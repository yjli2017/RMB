## Part 1: Light Schedule Verification

### Overview
The first and most critical step in circadian rhythm analysis is to verify the light schedule used during the experiment. This ensures that:
- Light cycles are properly synchronized across all monitors
- Expected 12:12 Light:Dark (LD) cycles are maintained
- Data quality is validated before proceeding with analysis
